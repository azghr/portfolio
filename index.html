Preface

This volume records the work of Muhammad Asghar Ali, a software engineer based in Pakistan who works primarily in Go, TypeScript and Python. It gathers, in order: contributions to open-source projects, selected public repositories, private work in progress, and professional experience.

The common thread is durable, well-built backend systems: APIs that hold their shape under load, workflows that do not lose their state, and tools that make other engineers faster.

Read in any order. The contents page will take you directly to any section.
